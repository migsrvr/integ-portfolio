Mediseena: Event-Driven Prescription Digitization with Multimodal AI Extraction and Human Review. A centralized web platform that converts photographs and scans of handwritten prescriptions into structured, reviewable medical records—combining client-side Canvas preprocessing, Tesseract.js OCR, schema-constrained Google Gemini extraction via Supabase Edge Functions, mandatory human verification, and PostgreSQL row-level security.
